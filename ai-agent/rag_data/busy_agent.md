# Busy Mode AI Agent (Auto-Responder)

When you are busy, an AI agent can chat with anyone who messages you and notify you when they need you.

## How to turn it on
1. Open Settings
2. In "Scheduled AI Auto-Responder Agent", switch on Auto-Responder Status
3. Keep "Use Smart AI Agent" on for AI replies, or switch it off to send a fixed message
4. Write a note for the agent (what you are doing, when you are free, what to tell people)
5. Optionally pick a start and end time, or use a quick preset (1 hr, 3 hrs, 8 hrs)
6. Click Save Settings

## What the agent does
- Introduces itself as your AI assistant and says you are busy and when you will be free
- Holds a real conversation: answers questions using your note and replies to small talk
- Never makes promises or commitments on your behalf
- Contacts see "Busy" next to your name and a typing indicator while the agent replies

## Getting notified
- If someone asks the agent to tell or notify you, or says it is urgent, the agent notifies you
- Contacts can also tap the "Notify" button under the agent's reply
- You get an in-app alert, a notification in the bell icon, and an email
- Repeat requests from the same person are limited to one notification every 10 minutes, unless it becomes urgent

## Fixed message mode
- With "Use Smart AI Agent" off, contacts receive your fixed message
- The fixed message is sent at most once every 5 minutes per contact

## Common issues
- No auto-reply: check that busy mode is on and the current time is inside your start and end time
- Replies look generic: the AI service may be waking up or unavailable; try again in a minute
